Four-layer feature module with a strict dependency direction (providers → data/ → core):
- `providers.dart` is the single entry point: it wires Riverpod providers (`paymentsRepositoryProvider`, `pendingPaymentStoreProvider`, `checkoutLauncherProvider`) and owns `PaymentSessionNotifier` (`StateNotifier<PaymentSessionState>`), which drives the three-phase flow initiate → verify → credit. It also exposes helper factories (`newPaymentReference`, `newPartnerTransId`, `normalizeMomoNumber`) and the `deliverPendingPayment` dispatcher that maps each `PendingPaymentKind` to a `ServicesRepository` call.
- `data/payments_repository.dart` defines the `PaymentsRepository` abstract interface; `data/remote_payments_repository.dart` implements it against `ApiClient` using `ApiEndpoints.advansisInitiate/query` and `ApiEndpoints.gtPayPay/status`, isolating the two rails (Advansis card checkout vs GTPay MoMo debit).
- `data/pending_payment.dart` models the durable purchase record (`PendingPayment` + `PendingPaymentKind` enum) with kind-specific factory constructors and `copyWith`; `data/pending_payment_store.dart` persists it in `SharedPreferences` while splitting sensitive fields (`token`, `momoNumber`) into `SecretStore` (Keystore/Keychain) via a `hydrate`/`save`/`load`/`clear` contract, including migration from legacy plaintext shape.
- `screens/payment_return_screen.dart` is the `lidapay://` deep-link target delegating verification to `paymentSessionProvider.handleReturn` and rendering receipts, shop success, or awaiting panels.
- `widgets/payment_awaiting_panel.dart` and `widgets/payment_method_selector.dart` are UI helpers consumed by screens elsewhere in the app.
Concurrency safety is built into the notifier: a `_generation` counter plus a single-flight `_verifying` future keyed by `pending.reference` prevents double-crediting on duplicated deep links or app resume, and GTBank MoMo polling uses an expiry window anchored to `PendingPayment.createdAt`.
