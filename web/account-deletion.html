Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to the LidaPay account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile and select Delete Account.
  3. Review the notice and confirm. The app opens a pre-filled email addressed to support.

Send the email to submit your request. Support may contact you to verify account ownership before completing deletion. Account deletion is permanent and ends access to LidaPay services. Withdraw or spend any remaining wallet balance and resolve pending payments or orders before requesting deletion.

Can't sign in?

Email us from the email address registered to your account. Include your registered phone number and ask us to delete your LidaPay account. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that you own the account. Never send your password, payment PIN or one-time code.

What happens to your information?

We delete or de-identify personal information that we are not required to keep. Transaction, payment, fraud-prevention and accounting records may be retained for periods required by applicable law or to resolve disputes and enforce legal rights. We do not state a fixed deletion or backup-erasure period here. You may contact us to ask about the status of a specific request.
