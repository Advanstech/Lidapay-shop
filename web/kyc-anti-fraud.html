Why verification may be requested

Identity and contact verification can help protect accounts and transactions. Verification may be required by LidaPay, a service or payment partner, or applicable law. The checks required may depend on the feature you use.

Information you may be asked to provide

Verification may include confirming your registered email address or phone number and completing account profile details. Additional checks may be requested where required by a payment/service partner or applicable law. We will not ask you to send your password, card PIN or one-time sign-in code to support.

Transaction review and account restrictions

We and our service partners may review account or transaction activity to identify suspected fraud, unauthorized use or service abuse. A transaction or account feature may be delayed or restricted while a concern is reviewed, subject to applicable law.

Delivery details

Provide a reachable delivery address and contact number for shop orders. The checkout uses the postal and contact address fields supported by the app. A courier may use the contact details you provide to coordinate delivery.
