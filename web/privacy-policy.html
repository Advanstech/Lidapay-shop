Privacy Policy

How LidaPay collects, uses and protects information

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Advansis Technologies ("LidaPay", "we", "us") handles information when you use the LidaPay mobile app and related services. LidaPay is operated from Accra, Greater Accra, Ghana.

Information we collect

The information depends on the features you use and includes:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, country, username and login credentials.
  • Profile and delivery information: address, city, region, digital address and location coordinates if you choose to obtain and save them.
  • Transactions and services: recipient phone numbers, airtime/data purchases, wallet activity, payment and transaction references, order details, delivery addresses, amounts and timestamps.
  • Device and app information: an app-scoped random device ID, device model/manufacturer, operating-system and app versions, locale, and a push-notification token.
  • Support information: messages and other details you choose to send to us.

Payment information is handled by the payment option you select. Do not send card PINs, passwords or one-time codes to support.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify contact details; provide wallet, payment, airtime/data and shop features; route and reconcile transactions; send account, security and service notifications; arrange deliveries; answer support requests; prevent misuse; meet legal obligations; and operate and maintain the service.

Location

If you choose a location-fill action, the app requests foreground location permission and obtains a location to help complete your profile or delivery address. The operating-system geocoding service may receive coordinates to return an address. Coordinates are included when you save profile location details; shop checkout submits the supported postal and contact address fields. The app does not request background location. You may deny permission, enter an address manually, or revoke permission in device settings.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • With our backend service at api.advansistechnologies.com to operate accounts, transactions and support.
  • With Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications.
  • With the payment provider selected at checkout and relevant telecom/top-up providers to process payments or deliver purchases. Recipient numbers and transaction details needed for delivery may be shared.
  • With a shop merchant and delivery/courier partner to fulfill an order, including the recipient name, phone number and delivery address.
  • With Gravatar if the app requests an email-derived profile image, and with platform geocoding services when you request address lookup.
  • With authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect users, the service or our legal rights.

Storage and security

The app communicates with our API over HTTPS and stores session tokens using platform secure storage (Android secure storage or the iOS Keychain). Optional biometric unlock uses the device authentication mechanism; the app does not receive your biometric template. We use authentication and operational safeguards to protect account information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and account deletion

We retain personal information while it is needed to provide your account and services. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete or de-identify personal information we are not required to keep. Transaction, payment, fraud-prevention and accounting records may be retained for periods required by applicable law or to resolve disputes and enforce legal rights. We do not state a fixed deletion or backup-erasure period here. See how to request account deletion.

Device storage, notifications and analytics

The app stores settings such as theme and onboarding state, a random app-scoped device ID, and protected session data on your device. It uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. The current mobile app dependency configuration does not include an advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. This mobile app does not use browser cookies. You can manage notification permission in device settings.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information, request account deletion, or raise a privacy concern. You can revoke location or notification permissions in device settings. Contact us to make a privacy request. Some records may need to be retained by law.

Children

LidaPay is not directed at children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app and the revised date will appear on this page.
