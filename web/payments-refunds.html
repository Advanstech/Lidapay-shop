This policy explains how to check payments and ask us to investigate a transaction. It applies to LidaPay purchases and shop orders, subject to applicable consumer law and the terms of the payment provider you select.

Before confirming a payment

Check the recipient, service or product, delivery details, final amount and any fee shown in the app. Your payment is processed by the selected payment provider. Provider processing times and terms may apply.

Pending payments and top-ups

A pending payment is not confirmation that a top-up or order was completed. Check the transaction status in the app before trying again to avoid duplicate payment. If you were charged but the service was not delivered, contact support with the transaction reference, amount and date. We will investigate with the relevant payment or service provider and arrange an eligible correction, redelivery or refund.

Lida Value transactions

Review the amount and destination before confirming a Lida Value use, send, sale or conversion. A transaction already completed by the applicable service or payment provider may not be reversible. If you believe a transaction was unauthorized or incorrect, contact support promptly.

Shop cancellation, returns and refunds

Cancellation eligibility may depend on whether an order has been dispatched. Return eligibility depends on product type, condition and applicable consumer law. Contact support as soon as possible with your order reference. Approved refunds are sent through the original payment route where possible; the time for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider. We will communicate any available status or reference.

Report an issue

Include your transaction or order reference, the date, amount and a brief description. Never send your password, card PIN or one-time code.
